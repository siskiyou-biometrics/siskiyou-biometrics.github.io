Independent review, design, and strategy for the forest inventory and biometric systems supporting timberland investment and forest management decisions.

Practice

In my time working in the timberland investment industry, I led the biometrics and inventory work supporting institutional forestland investment across four countries, and I rebuilt the growth, merchandising, and estimation systems that key investment decisions and ground operations both run on. An acquisition, once closed, is final: the numbers underneath it deserve scrutiny before the deal, not after. The experience is wider than one industry: small area estimation research, rFIA (an open-source tool the field still uses), and companies of my own. Siskiyou Biometrics is the independent practice founded on all of it. I tell clients what their data can and cannot support, and I design the methods and systems that do not exist off the shelf.

Hunter Stanke

Who we serve

The practice is small by design, built for timberland investors and managers, conservation organizations that acquire and steward working forests, federal and state agencies, universities and research groups, and forest-tech firms building the field's next tools.
